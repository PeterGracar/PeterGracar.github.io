About

I am a Lecturer in Probability in the School of Mathematics at the University of Leeds.

I work on random graphs with a spatial structure and on percolation with dependencies: scale-free geometric networks and the random walks and contact processes that live on them, Boolean models whose grains move or have heavy-tailed sizes, and Lipschitz surfaces in dependent percolation. The research page lists my papers and coauthors; several papers come with an interactive simulation. The teaching page lists the modules I lead, tutor or supervise this year.
